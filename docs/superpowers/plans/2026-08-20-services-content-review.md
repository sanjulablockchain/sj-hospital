# Services content review checklist

**Purpose:** before the new `/services` section goes live, every clinical claim on it needs a clinician or hospital administrator to sign off. This document lists all 36 service pages plus the shared `/services` index, tells you where each page's copy came from, and points at the specific lines worth checking. It does not repeat every sentence of copy — for the full wording, open the page itself at the URL given.

**How the copy was written:** 11 pages started from an existing design reference and had their copy adapted; the other 25 were written from scratch for this project. Neither group is more trustworthy than the other — the reference's own copy was partly invented, so adapted pages need the same scrutiny as the fresh ones.

---

## 1. What to read first

If you only have ten minutes, check these — being wrong here matters more than anywhere else on the site:

- **Emergency capability** (`/services/accident-emergency`) — the page states on-call surgical and anaesthetic teams and a theatre are kept ready at every hour, so emergency surgery happens on site rather than after a transfer. Confirm this is genuinely true around the clock, not just during normal working hours.
- **ICU scope** (`/services/intensive-critical-care`) — described as continuous monitoring with daily consultant rounds and "a small, fixed group of beds" per nurse, deliberately without stating a nurse-to-bed ratio. Confirm the unit can actually deliver what's described, and decide whether a ratio should be added.
- **What is not offered on site** — three pages are written to say a service is *not* available here and routes the patient elsewhere: `radiology` (no CT or MRI; referral to a partner centre), `nephrology` (no dialysis mentioned at all), and the dental service that used to appear in the design reference has been removed entirely (no dentist on staff). If any of these has changed, the copy needs to change with it.
- **The one price on the site** — `/services/inpatient-rooms` states rooms are "available from 10,000 LKR a night" because the hospital's own existing site already states this figure. Every other one of the 36 pages is deliberately price-free. Confirm the figure is still current; do not let it spread to other pages.
- **Fertility eligibility** — `/services/fertility` is written so it neither confirms nor rules out care for a single patient without a partner. This is flagged as unresolved and needs a definitive answer before launch, not just a copy tweak.

## 3. Deliberately narrow entries

These five pages were written conservatively on purpose. If the hospital can confirm a fuller scope, the copy should say more — but only after someone signs off on the specifics, not before.

- **`neurosurgery`** — covers assessment, imaging-led planning and post-operative follow-up only. No procedure list is named anywhere on the page. If the service performs specific named operations, the page currently understates it.
- **`fertility`** — no techniques are named, and there is no outcome or success-rate claim anywhere. Eligibility for a single patient without a partner versus a couple is left unresolved in the copy and needs a definitive answer — this is the one open question on the whole site that most needs an answer before launch, not just a wording fix.
- **`haematology`** — blood film and bone marrow findings are described as reported "through our histopathology service." This workflow claim needs confirming; if haematology reports these itself, the page should say so instead.
- **`nephrology`** — no dialysis is claimed anywhere on the page. Confirm whether the hospital offers dialysis and, if so, whether it should be added.
- **`radiology`** — CT and MRI are stated as arranged by referral to a partner imaging centre, not performed on site. Confirm this is still true; if the hospital has since installed either, this is the most consequential single fact to update on the entire site.

## 4. Deliberate omissions — do not reinstate

The design reference this project drew from made a number of claims that were removed by decision during the build. If a future edit is tempted to restore any of the reference's original wording, check this list first — these were cut for a reason, not by oversight:

- **Dental services** — the reference included a dental page; there is no dentist on staff, so it was dropped entirely.
- **Health-check package pricing** — the reference priced three tiers at 9,500 / 18,500 / 32,000 LKR. These figures were confirmed not real and removed; the packages section on `/services` is intentionally reworded ("Priced up front" → a heading that doesn't promise a price) and carries no numbers.
- **Cardiac screening "same-day interpretation"** claim — removed from `/services/cardiac-screening`.
- **Radiology "lower radiation dose than older film systems"** comparison — removed from `/services/radiology`.
- **Echocardiogram "around half an hour"** duration claim — removed from `/services/cardiac-screening`.
- **Lab turnaround figures** — "48 to 72 h" for microbiology and "3 to 5 days" for histopathology — removed from `/services/laboratory`; the page now says "same day for most tests" without the specific exceptions.
- **Wound-care "weekly reviews"** cadence — removed from `/services/dermatology`; the schedule is now described only as agreed at the first visit.
- **Five A&E response-time promises** ("within minutes" / "straight away") — removed from `/services/accident-emergency`.
- **ICU "without delay" transfer claim** — removed from `/services/intensive-critical-care`.

## 5. Index-page items, not tied to one service

A handful of open questions live on the `/services` index page itself rather than on a single service page:

- **Comforts list** (free parking, free wifi, cafeteria, prayer room, card & bank-transfer payment, wheelchair access, quiet visiting hours, meals to dietary orders, attendant space) — confirm every item is still true.
- **Admissions billing and insurance framing** — "a written estimate is given before treatment starts," cash/card/bank-transfer accepted, insurance paperwork prepared at the desk. Confirm this matches actual admissions practice.
